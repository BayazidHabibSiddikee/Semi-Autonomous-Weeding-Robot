# AI-Based Elevator Fault Detection

## Tags

`IoT` `Predictive` `Sensors` `Maintenance`

## Problem

Elevators receive scheduled maintenance even when fine. Some faults develop between inspections.

## Solution

Monitor vibration, motor current, door movement, and travel time. Predict faults before breakdowns.

## Society Impact

- Millions use elevators daily
- Hospitals, offices, apartments depend on them
- Early detection improves reliability

## Environmental Impact

- Longer equipment life
- Fewer replacement parts
- Reduced maintenance travel
- Lower electricity use

## Feasibility

- ✅ Vibration + current sensors
- ✅ Can simulate with small elevator model
- ✅ ML for anomaly detection
