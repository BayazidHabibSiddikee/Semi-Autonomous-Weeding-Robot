# Intelligent Warehouse Monitoring System

## Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐

## Tags

`Object Detection` `Inventory` `Safety` `Computer Vision` `IoT`

## Problem

Inventory mistakes cost companies billions. Workers face safety risks from forklifts and restricted areas.

## Solution

Monitor inventory and worker safety automatically.

### Features

- **Object detection** — track inventory items
- **Safety helmet detection** — ensure PPE compliance
- **Restricted area monitoring** — alert on unauthorized access
- **Inventory counting** — automated stock levels
- **Forklift tracking** — prevent collisions

## Society Impact

- Faster deliveries
- Lower product prices
- Safer workplaces
- Better inventory management

## Environmental Impact

- Less overproduction
- Fewer expired/lost products
- Less waste and lower resource consumption
