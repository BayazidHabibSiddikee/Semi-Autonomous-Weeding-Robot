# Predictive Battery Health Monitoring

## Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Highest Societal Impact)

## Tags

`Battery` `ML` `IoT` `Sustainability` `Electric Vehicles`

## Problem

Batteries degrade over time. Users don't know condition until performance drops or failure occurs.

Affects: electric vehicles, solar energy systems, UPS, consumer electronics.

## Solution

Estimate battery health and remaining useful life from voltage, current, temperature, and charge/discharge history.

### Features

- State of Health (SoH) estimation
- Remaining Useful Life (RUL) prediction
- Anomaly detection in charging patterns
- Temperature-based degradation tracking
- Dashboard with battery status

## Society Impact

- More reliable electric vehicles
- Better renewable energy storage
- Lower maintenance costs
- Reduced risk of unexpected failures

## Environmental Impact — Biggest Sustainability Impact

If batteries last 20% longer:

- Fewer batteries manufactured
- Less mining of lithium, cobalt, nickel
- Less hazardous battery waste in landfills
- Renewable energy systems become more effective

## Feasibility

- ✅ Battery datasets available (NASA, CALCE)
- ✅ Python + scikit-learn for ML
- ✅ Can start with Li-ion cell testing
- ✅ Strong SDG alignment (Goal 7, 9, 12)
