# AI-Based Laboratory Safety Monitoring

## Tags

`Safety` `Computer Vision` `PPE Detection` `IoT`

## Problem

People forget safety rules: no goggles, no gloves, chemical spills ignored, unsafe behavior.

## Solution

AI monitors the laboratory and warns users.

### Features

- PPE compliance detection (goggles, gloves, lab coat)
- Unsafe behavior recognition
- Chemical spill detection
- Restricted zone monitoring
- Real-time alerts

## Society Impact

- Fewer accidents
- Better safety culture
- Lower medical costs
- Safer students and workers

## Environmental Impact

- Quick spill detection reduces chemical pollution
- Hazardous waste handled sooner

## Feasibility

- ✅ YOLO for PPE detection
- ✅ Can simulate lab environment
- ✅ Strong safety narrative
